# Often Confused pilot — source and validation notes

Prepared 2026-10-29 from the existing 12-item / 5-group asset. The expanded asset has **54 distinct items and 24 groups**. This is a curated beginner practice library, not an official or complete HSK syllabus. All new examples and four-language translations are original editorial content; dictionary entries support selected readings and senses, not authorship or approval of the example sentences.

## Primary educator and official dictionary sources

- [University of Oxford CTCFL: Pinyin pronunciation notes](https://www.ctcfl.ox.ac.uk/materials_pinyin-notes/): consonant examples distinguish j/q/x and z/c/s from zh/ch/sh; the tones section gives four full tones plus an unmarked neutral tone. Tone changes explain why dictionary tone marks and connected speech can differ. Our notes avoid English spelling approximations.
- [University of Oxford CTCFL: audio practice](https://www.ctcfl.ox.ac.uk/materials_pinyin/): educator audio resources for initials, finals and tone practice. Appropriate reference for hearing contrasts; no claim of identical audio timing or voice to this app.
- [Taiwan Ministry of Education: 哪裡](https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=45213): nǎ lǐ, interrogative location sense.
- [Taiwan Ministry of Education: 那裡](https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=12173&la=1&powerMode=1): nà lǐ, that place. This official source supports the full-tone variant. The pilot selects **nàli** for 那里 following CC-CEDICT; its group note in all four languages explicitly accepts full-tone nàlǐ. The region-dependent variation is not a learner error.

## Dictionary source and selected senses

[CC-CEDICT via MDBG downloadable dictionary](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/export/cedict/cedict_1_0_ts_utf-8_mdbg.txt.gz) was checked against the exact simplified-character entries for all 54 items. [MDBG dictionary interface](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary) gives direct per-word links below. CC-CEDICT is a community dictionary, distinguished here from the academic and official sources above. No copied dictionary examples or AI explanations are used. Selected dictionary readings are converted from numbered tones to tone marks and standard word grouping.

Selected claims: 厕所 cèsuǒ is the complete word for toilet; 所 alone is not taught as toilet. 被子 bèizi and 杯子 bēizi have neutral 子. 要 yào uses the wanting sense, 雨 yǔ the noun rain, 月 yuè month, 元 yuán currency unit, 粗 cū coarse texture, 包 bāo bag, 饱 bǎo satiated, 清 qīng clear water, 鸡 jī the bird. Other dictionary readings/senses of polyphonic characters are deliberately outside this pilot.

## Editorial and validation decisions

- The first 12 complete item objects remain byte-for-value identical after JSON parsing; their scenes, text, readings and sentences were preserved. Original five groups retain every existing field and gain category only.
- All 42 new scenes use their exact item IDs. Existing `do` continues to use scene `cook`.
- Four-language meanings, translations, group titles and notes are present in EN/FR/RU/TR. All 54 item IDs and 24 group IDs are unique; all group references resolve; no item is unreferenced.
- `sound` allows multiple phonetic differences in a set; it does not assert homophony. `homophone` identifies the main context-dependent distinction in 做/坐; the original group note separately identifies 走. The `near-enter-far` note distinguishes 近/进 homophony from 远 as a semantic opposite. `want-have` is a meaning association, not a minimal sound pair.
- Dictionary tones are retained in sentence Pinyin, including third tones subject to normal speech sandhi. Neutral syllables are unmarked. This spelling convention does not prescribe a full dipping third tone in every sentence position.
- No unresolved selected-sense readings. Known accepted variation: 那里 nàli / nàlǐ, disclosed above and in the multilingual group note.
- No repository, Git, build, release, deployment or server state was changed by this asset delivery.


Root editorial checks: 找 is taught as look for, without claiming that the object has been found; the illustration has no success checkmark. 粗 uses a thick-rope example to match its image. 元 uses an explicit cup-of-tea price sentence.

Auxiliary clickable vocabulary: 绳子 shéngzi (rope) and 绳 shéng (rope/cord), checked against the same CC-CEDICT entries. Both have local four-language definitions and individual recordings; these support the thick-rope sentence and are not counted as additional comparison cards.
