# Teaching references for Atlas

## Chinese Decoded: the 莫 family

Reviewed on 23 October 2026: [One meaning to unlock 10 Chinese characters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlUN5XEj8Aw). Review used the English transcript and sampled visual sequences, not a claim to have watched the full 24-minute playback.

Useful teaching patterns:

- Keep a shared component visually stable as the surrounding component changes.
- Give one character a memorable scene, then immediately show whole words and their uses.
- Contrast members of a family so learners retrieve the distinction rather than only recognize a repeated shape.
- Revisit the same family through pictures, meaning, sound and independent retrieval.

Use these organizational ideas to create original Atlas lessons; do not copy the video's illustrations, narration or transcript into the app.

Accuracy boundary: the video proposes a unifying “cover” concept for the 莫 family and derives meanings from it. Treat this as an author's explanatory/mnemonic model until each historical claim has supporting evidence. Semantic and phonetic functions need character-specific labels; an attractive story is not sufficient evidence of etymology. [Dong Chinese on 莫](https://www.dong-chinese.com/wiki/莫) describes an original sunset image and a later phonetic loan for its negative meaning. [模](https://www.dong-chinese.com/wiki/模) and [慕](https://www.dong-chinese.com/wiki/慕) are described as phonosemantic compounds, with 莫 providing a sound clue. A component can have more than one role in a particular character; do not promise that one meaning predicts an entire family.

Potential later lesson: an optional discovery family around 莫, clearly separating verified origin notes, modern shape, approximate sound and memory scenes. Keep advanced vocabulary out of the beginner HSK and intensive-course schedules unless explicitly authored for those courses. No 莫-family content was added as part of the listening-practice release.
