ChineseOS dictionary resources

headwords.json.gz is a Han-only simplified/traditional headword index derived
from CC-CEDICT by its contributors, distributed by MDBG.
Source: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The derived index is distributed under the same license. It contains no new
curriculum identities. English definitions are retrieved through the fixed
CC-CEDICT bulk-data source and cached on the reader's device.

characters.json.gz contains selected dictionary fields from Make Me a Hanzi.
Source: https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
Existing license notices: /licenses/makemeahanzi-COPYING.txt and
/licenses/makemeahanzi-dictionary-LGPL.txt
Modern radicals/decomposition are dataset descriptions, not historical claims.

Reproduction script: scripts/build-dictionary-resources.py in the public source
repository. Snapshot digests and counts: data/dictionary-sources.json.
